  - Haptics on send/stop, suggestion taps, sheet actions

- **Terminal native elevation** — create or refine `src/app/(tabs)/terminal.android.tsx`:
  - Inset terminal pane, M3 chips (`command-chip.android.tsx`, `mode-picker.android.tsx`)

- **Interactions audit**:
  - `expo-haptics`: impactAsync(Light) on taps, notificationAsync on success/error/confirm
